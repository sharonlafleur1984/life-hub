# Persona: the default coordinator

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

The person the Life Hub is built for. She is an archetype, not a real person: her name is made up, and nothing here identifies a real household.

## Who she is

**Jen, 40s, single working mom of two teenagers.**

- Shares custody with the kids' dad, so schedules, pickups and shared costs go back and forth between two homes.
- In a career change, looking for her next role while freelancing and learning new skills.
- One of her kids has a school support plan, which means meetings, paperwork and deadlines on top of the usual school load.
- Runs the whole house alone: a dog, a car, a side rental, bills, appointments and a volunteer shift.
- Uses Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, a to-do app and a budgeting app, and none of them talk to each other.

## What a day looks like

She wakes up already behind. Before coffee she has scanned three inboxes, a family group chat and two calendars, trying to spot the one thing that can't wait. Most of it can. The one that can't is usually buried in an email she skimmed last week.

## What she needs

- **One short list each morning** of what needs her today, with the work already done.
- **Nothing due by surprise.** Renewals, deadlines and payments show up weeks ahead, not the day of.
- **One decision at a time.** When everything is in front of her at once, she freezes and nothing moves.
- **Options, not homework.** Two or three choices with a recommendation, not a blank page.

## What gets in her way

- **She is the coordinator for everything.** Nobody else tracks the recurring stuff, so if she forgets, it doesn't happen.
- **Information is scattered** across apps, emails and texts, and finding it takes longer than doing it.
- **Too much at once.** Long walls of text and long lists cost her the decision they were meant to help with.
- **Tools that pretend to help.** A button that leads nowhere, or a reminder with a made-up deadline, costs her trust in the whole system.
- **Private things need to stay private.** Money, health and her kids' details can't end up somewhere others can see.

## What success looks like

She reads one short morning report, thinks "that is exactly what I needed and I did not have to ask," and spends the time she got back on her kids, her work or herself.

## How the Life Hub answers her

| Her need                | What the Life Hub does                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One short list          | A daily read of email, calendar and household boards, curated down to what needs a decision |
| Nothing due by surprise | Every recurring obligation surfaces 30 days ahead ([Roadmap](Roadmap))                      |
| One decision at a time  | Specialists do the legwork and bring her two or three options with a recommendation         |
| Trust                   | Buttons are real or absent, and logistics are shown only when they came from a real source  |
| Privacy                 | Money, health and credentials never get an action button, and household data stays private  |
