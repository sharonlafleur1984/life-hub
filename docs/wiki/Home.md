# Life Hub: Dashboard

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

A household operating system run by Claude, with Notion as the system of record. One house manager coordinates eight life-area specialists, each a Claude skill. The owner should only see what needs her, with the work already done.

**Hypothesis:** a household can be run by a small staff of AI specialists that read widely, propose carefully, and let one coordinator make every write, so the owner's attention goes only to decisions.

**Problem to solve:** nothing tracks the recurring obligations of a household in one place, and the owner ends up as the coordinator for everything.

**What does success look like?** The owner reads one short morning report, thinks "that is exactly what I needed and I did not have to ask," and nothing about the household arrives late or as a surprise.
